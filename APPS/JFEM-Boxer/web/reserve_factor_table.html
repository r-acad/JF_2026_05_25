Reserve factors

Connecting to Study…

This table follows the load-case selection and envelope method in Results → Stress Report. Change those controls in the Study window; this tab updates automatically and is read-only. Each reserve factor is the isotropic material’s allowable von Mises stress divided by the property-region stress envelope. Red means below 1, blue means above 1, and 1 is the neutral threshold. Reserve factors are dimensionless.

For each property, the selected statistic is calculated within each physical SOL101 or fully converged SOL106 load case; the largest case value governs. “Average below percentile” discards only element peaks above that case’s cutoff, retaining ties. Averages weight elements equally. These ratios do not predict nonlinear collapse loads.

Panel and component cells use recorded property ownership. Properties spanning multiple locations are shown once as shared regions; their reserve factor is never presented as a separate local result for each bay. Imported models without lifting-surface ownership use the property list. Missing cases, stale geometry, incomplete recovery, absent allowables and unsupported laminate criteria remain explicitly unassessed. They are not replaced by zero and do not contribute to the color range.

Displayed numbers have at most five significant digits. Stress units are included in column headings and value details. Exports contain one row per property for the current group and filter, with table-view metadata, governing cases and assessment notes. If the Study closes, the last received report remains available and is clearly marked disconnected.
