Sixteen unedited, high-resolution screenshots from the running app, on a white background. Click an image to inspect the original pixels. Full views are 4728 × 2832 pixels; interface views are 4800 × 3000 pixels.

These images show the uniform isotropic CRM adaptation and its five recorded modes. Its frequencies are historical regression data. The separate published uCRM9 case retains the released stiffnesses, concentrated masses and loads for an independent comparison.

The model contains shells only, so there are no 3D bars to display. The physical-thickness views use 20 mm. Stiffener effects are smeared; no separate stiffeners have been invented. Download all captions and image hashes.
