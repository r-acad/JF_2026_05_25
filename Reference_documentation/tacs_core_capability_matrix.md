# JFEM TACS Core Capability Matrix

This note tracks the JFEM-core work required to approach the formulation and
sensitivity breadth of TACS. It intentionally excludes Python, pyTACS, GUI,
packaging, MPI/distributed runtime, and external workflow tooling. The scope is
only the Julia solver core: element formulations, constitutive models,
responses, analysis routes, and sensitivities.

## Status Legend

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Implemented | Present in the JFEM TACS-formulation backend and covered by route guards. |
| Partial | Present for a narrow shell/material/design-variable subset. |
| Missing | Not implemented in the JFEM TACS-formulation backend. |
| Deferred | Useful TACS capability, but not part of the next core tranche. |

## Current Implemented Slice

The current TACS-formulation backend is a guarded residual-first shell slice
with initial SOL101 rod stiffness, SOL101 CBAR/CBEAM beam stiffness and sizing
sensitivities, SOL103 rod modal, SOL103 CBAR/CBEAM beam modal and sizing
sensitivities plus SOL103 CBAR/CBEAM modal offsets and pin releases and
SOL105 CBAR/CBEAM buckling offsets and pin releases, SOL103 varying-station
CBAR/CBEAM modal stiffness/mass, SOL105 varying-station CBAR/CBEAM
geometric-stiffness,
SOL101/SOL103 CBAR/CBEAM beam shape sensitivities, SOL105
CBAR/CBEAM beam buckling sensitivities, rod and beam geometric-stiffness
operators, and mixed shell-plus-rod SOL105 buckling sensitivity slices plus
SOL101 and SOL103 scalar/bushing spring stiffness, CONM1 full-matrix modal
mass/sensitivity, SOL101 CBAR/CBEAM axial and bending stress recovery,
SOL101 CBAR/CBEAM PLOAD1 beam loads, SOL101 CBAR/CBEAM GRAV/RFORCE
load-only material-density sensitivities, SOL101 CROD/CONROD/CBAR/CBEAM
TEMP(LOAD) axial thermal load sensitivities, SOL101 CBAR/CBEAM static offsets and pin
releases, SOL103 CBAR/CBEAM modal offsets and pin releases, SOL105 CBAR/CBEAM
buckling offsets and pin releases, and scalar modal-mass slices.

SOL103 CBAR/CBEAM beam modal assembly is guarded with a two-DOF cantilever bending oscillator that checks the lumped translational and rotary beam mass terms plus eigenvalue/frequency against the closed-form 2x2 generalized eigenproblem. SOL103 CBAR/CBEAM beam modal material sensitivities are guarded for MAT1 `material_E` and `material_RHO` against full re-solves, and SOL103 beam sizing sensitivities are guarded for `beam_I1`, `beam_I2`, and `beam_area` eigenvalue/frequency derivatives against full re-solves. SOL101/SOL103 CBAR/CBEAM beam shape sensitivities are guarded for end-node `node_coord` length perturbations against closed-form static cantilever, structural-mass, and two-DOF modal derivatives; SOL105 CBAR/CBEAM buckling shape sensitivities are guarded for the same end-node length perturbation against full re-solves and a closed-form reduced buckling pencil. SOL105 CBAR/CBEAM beam buckling is guarded for constant-section compressed beam-only decks through the native `native_residual_first_cbar_cbeam_operator` geometric-stiffness route; the guard checks axial-force recovery, both UZ/RY and UY/RZ geometric-stiffness entries, the first generalized eigenvalue, selected-mode `beam_I1`/`beam_I2` and MAT1 `material_E` gradients, selected-mode `beam_area`/`beam_J` invariance derivatives, and single-mode KS `beam_I1`/`beam_I2`/`beam_area`/`beam_J` gradients against full re-solves. Public structural-mass value/gradient support includes CBAR/CBEAM scalar beam mass plus `beam_area`, MAT1 `material_RHO`, and length-coordinate derivatives. Shared SOL101 CBAR/CBEAM stress recovery is guarded on the TACS route for axial force/stress/strain plus PBARL BAR bending end moments and surface stresses; SOL101 CBAR/CBEAM KS von-Mises beam stress sensitivities are guarded for constant-section `beam_I2` bending and `beam_area` axial stress derivatives, including MAT1 `material_E` stress cancellation, against full plus/minus re-solves; offset/release recovery applies PA/PB condensation so released-end moments are zero in parser-backed static decks. Guarded SOL101 CBAR/CBEAM PLOAD1 support covers local FY/FZ distributed loads with LE/FR scaling, partial linearly varying spans, static displacements, and fixed-end-corrected recovered root shear/moment. SOL101 CBAR/CBEAM GRAV material-density load-only sensitivity is guarded for static compliance and scalar displacement against full plus/minus density re-solves and a closed-form cantilever gravity derivative. SOL101 CBAR/CBEAM RFORCE material-density load-only sensitivity is guarded with a consistent two-node inertial load against full density re-solves and a closed-form axial centrifugal response for compliance, scalar displacement, and single-entry KS displacement. SOL101 CBAR/CBEAM TEMP(LOAD) axial thermal load sensitivities are guarded for MAT1 `material_E`, `material_ALPHA`, and `material_TREF` compliance, scalar displacement, and single-entry KS displacement against full re-solves and a closed-form axial thermal displacement. SOL101 CBAR/CBEAM beam offsets and pin releases are guarded for offset-only, release-only, and combined offset/release static decks against the legacy assembler and solver, including parser-backed CBAR and CBEAM offset/release input. SOL103 CBAR/CBEAM modal offsets and pin releases are guarded on parser-backed offset-only, release-only, and combined offset/release decks; the guard verifies TACS-owned K/M assembly, offset mass perturbations, release stiffness perturbations, and first modal eigenvalues against an independent reduced generalized eigensolve. SOL105 CBAR/CBEAM buckling offsets and pin releases are guarded on parser-backed offset-only, release-only, and combined offset/release decks; the guard verifies TACS K/Kg parity with the existing parity backend and first buckling load factors against an independent reduced buckling pencil. The current beam slice still rejects distributed/follower beam loads beyond PLOAD1, GRAV, RFORCE, and TEMP(LOAD), broader stress/failure sensitivities, broader off-axis/multi-node shape sensitivities, and varying PBEAM/PBEAML offset/release, PLOAD1 fixed-end station stress recovery, and sensitivity semantics until those are separately guarded. |
| Spring elements | Partial | CELAS1, CELAS2, and CBUSH/PBUSH diagonal stiffness is guarded for SOL101 using direct, PELAS-backed, and PBUSH-backed springs against closed-form displacement checks. SOL103 spring-plus-point/scalar-mass modal oscillators are guarded for CELAS1/CONM2, CELAS2/CMASS2, and CBUSH/CMASS1/PMASS against closed-form `k/m` eigenvalues, `dLambda/dK = 1/m` stiffness sensitivities, and `point_mass` sensitivities with `dLambda/dm = -k/m^2`. SOL103 CONM1 full-matrix modal mass is guarded with a coupled two-DOF oscillator that checks off-diagonal `M12/M21` assembly and both eigenvalues; the same guard checks CONM1 full-matrix `point_mass` modal derivatives for diagonal `M11`/`M22` terms and off-diagonal `M12/M21` terms selected by `component_pairs`. SOL103 CONM2 rotational inertia has guarded `point_inertia` modal derivatives for principal `I11` and off-diagonal `I21` terms on rotational spring oscillators. Public structural-mass value/gradient support now includes CONM2, CMASS2, and PMASS-backed CMASS1 scalar masses with `dMass/dpoint_mass = 1`. SOL101 line-element static response sensitivities are guarded for `spring_stiffness` and PBUSH `bush_stiffness` on compliance, scalar displacement, and single-entry KS displacement. Spring damping, PBUSH coupling/orientation terms, broader point-inertia response semantics, SOL105 spring semantics, and broader sensitivities remain missing. |
| Unsupported element rejection | Implemented | Solids, unsupported mass variants, RBE/MPC, and other unsupported groups fail fast in the TACS route; rods fail fast outside the guarded SOL101 stiffness, SOL103 modal, and mixed-shell SOL105 geometric-stiffness slices; beams fail fast outside the guarded SOL101 constant-section stiffness, guarded SOL101 varying-station stiffness, SOL103 modal, and SOL105 constant-section buckling slices. Beam offsets and pin releases are accepted only for the guarded SOL101 static, SOL103 modal, and SOL105 buckling slices and rejected for SOL106; varying PBEAM/PBEAML station data remain rejected outside the guarded no-offset/no-release SOL101 stiffness, SOL101 stress recovery, SOL103 modal, and SOL105 buckling geometric-stiffness paths. Springs fail fast outside the guarded SOL101/SOL103 slices. |

These are the core formulation capabilities still missing or incomplete versus
the TACS core taxonomy.

SOL103 varying PBEAM/PBEAML modal stiffness/mass is guarded for the same straight no-offset/no-release CBAR/CBEAM slice by station condensation plus Guyan-reduced station mass. SOL105 varying PBEAM/PBEAML geometric stiffness is guarded for the same straight no-offset/no-release CBAR/CBEAM slice by segment axial-force recovery and station `Kg` condensation. ## Recommended Implementation Order

1. Kernelize the current shell backend without changing numerical behavior.
2. Add a JFEM-core response/function abstraction with value, state derivative,
   design derivative, and coordinate derivative contracts.
3. Broaden thermal load sensitivity beyond the current guarded axial
   TEMP(LOAD) line-element slice.
4. Broaden SOL200-lite buckling policy beyond the current shell sizing subset,
   including richer objective/constraint combinations.
5. Add remaining shell constitutive breadth: richer PCOMP/composite variants
   and failure APIs.
6. Finish line-element ownership: broader rod/spring sensitivities,
   broader SOL105 line sensitivity coverage,
   and remaining beam loads beyond PLOAD1/GRAV/RFORCE/TEMP(LOAD),
   broader stress/failure sensitivity, broader off-axis/multi-node shape,
   and varying PBEAM offset/stress/sensitivity breadth.
7. Add solids and plane-stress elements.
8. Add remaining spring/mass support: damping, point-inertia response
   semantics, coupled/oriented PBUSH terms, SOL105 spring semantics, and
   RBE/MPC-style support.
9. Add thermal and coupled thermo-structural kernels.

The near-term priority is not breadth for its own sake. The highest-value path
is to make the existing shell optimization core complete, derivative-checked,
and easy to extend, then plug new element families into the same contracts.
